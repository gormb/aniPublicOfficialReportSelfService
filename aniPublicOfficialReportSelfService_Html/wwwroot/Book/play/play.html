Play
    A playground for AI with tools to make, improve and enjoy books at different semantic zoom and detail levels: Each level has a guiding line for the reader and a set of features for the AI to apply. The levels are pl0..pl3, then pl4a/pl4b, pl5a/pl5b, pl6a/pl6b (a = text spine, b = page spine), with pl_dev_0 for development notes.

    Structure Book Shelf › Book Copy › Main Chapter › Sub Chapter — which splits into
    • Page → Line → Media Form (the presentation)
    • Paragraph → Sentence → Word (the text)
    Bind: a work may be published in several binds — a bind is the physical book, one of a series. A bind is its own book on the shelf: its own concept, folder and file (b/{book}/b_{lang}_{edition}.md), its own chapter sequence, its own start and its own numbering. So no extra dimension is needed anywhere — the copy at pl1 stays language × edition.
    The spines never cross: the page carries its lines and their media, a word ends the text spine.
    One source line = one paragraph; a line that resumes a sentence cut by a page marker or a line break joins the same paragraph, which can span pages (pn–pn2).
    A page's lines are word wrapped at the golden-ratio measure (≈66 characters) at 1.15 line spacing, so every line of a page can be addressed – the level a gaze maps to when eye tracking comes.
    Media Form is multi-modal – Foreground, Background, Sound (Spotify Play), Motion – one row per modality, drawn in the page.
    Hierarchy is data: parents in books.play.up, names/text/features in books.play.LV. The nav shows the selected node and its leaves, ⬆ goes to the parent node, 🫲/🫱 walk the siblings.
    Levels: pl0 shelf, pl1 copy (the physical book: bind × language × edition, where a bind is its own book), pl2 main chapter, pl3 sub chapter (the fork: pages │ paragraphs), pl4b page, pl5b line, pl6b media form, pl4a paragraph, pl5a sentence, pl6a word.
